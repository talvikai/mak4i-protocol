## 1. Status

**What this revision is.** Version 0.2 reconciles the unpublished v0.1
working draft (2026-09) with protocol v0.2 —

It is written jointly with the sibling draft [MAK-0003](MAK-0003.md)
(F-2) under one shared boundary (§3, S1).

**What changed from v0.1** (details in Appendix B):

- Open conflicts and integrity errors are carried as such. v0.1 assumed
  its input was "already conflict-resolved"; that is wrong under
  MAK-0004 Part A.
- Existence disclosure follows MAK-0004 §A6.4: `hidden_candidate_count`
  is disclosed. v0.1's blanket non-disclosure default is dropped.
- Provenance is the authenticated MAK-0006 §7 object, not MAK-0001
  `author`.
- Lifecycle uses `active` / `superseded` / `withdrawn` for records and
  `active` / `deprecated` / `withdrawn` for distributable artifacts.
- "Context package" now names only this standard's output. The
  `mak4i_get_current` result is the "current knowledge result". The
  MAK-0003 output is the "applicability result".
- Authorization is MAK-0006, not "a future access-control standard".
- MAK-0009 inputs are added: inherited distributable artifacts and
  effective rules (a required category).
- v0.1's proposed normative sections are no longer normative. What is
  agreed is in §3. Everything else is an open item (§4) or a design note
  (Appendix A). v0.1's conformance list is removed.

**What it is not.** It is not accepted, not complete and not
implemented. It defines no schema, no tool, no CLI command and no
conformance criteria. Against the
[criteria](../ROADMAP.md#criteria-for-mak-0002-mak-0003-and-mak-0007)
for F-1/F-2, its Settled design meets criteria 1–9, 11 and 12. For

## 2. Purpose and scope

MAK-0002 defines the **context package**: the structure in which MAK4I
knowledge that applies to a piece of work is packaged for an AI system,
and the rules for building it. MAK-0003 decides *what applies*; MAK-0002
decides *how it is packaged* — ordering, size, representation,
partiality and explanation.

"Context injection" in this standard means **preparing and assembling**
that package. It does not mean delivering it into an AI session (system
prompt, tool result, MCP resource, file). Delivery is out of scope.

**In scope (all open except where §3 says otherwise):**

- The categories a context package can carry, including conflicts,
  integrity errors, effective rules and inherited organization knowledge.
- How the applicability result (MAK-0003) becomes a context package.
- Ordering and priority of items.
- Current versus historical presentation.
- Embedded versus referenced content.
- Size budgets and reduction.
- Preserving provenance, authorization outcomes and disclosure limits.
- Partial packages, unavailable references and uncertain items.
- Explaining why an item is present.
- Identifying a package and its format version.

**Out of scope (non-goals):**

- Deciding applicability or relevance ([MAK-0003](MAK-0003.md)).
- Detecting or resolving conflicts ([MAK-0004](MAK-0004.md)).
- Lifecycle, supersession and integrity ([MAK-0005](MAK-0005.md)).
- Authorization decisions ([MAK-0006](MAK-0006.md)).
- Computing effective rules or inheritance ([MAK-0009](MAK-0009.md)).
- Delivery into an AI session, transports and wire formats.
- Guaranteeing that an AI system follows the package
  ([MAK-0009 §4.9](MAK-0009.md#4-rules-enforcement-and-inheritance)).
- Cross-organization knowledge and public registries
  ([MAK-0007](MAK-0007.md), Draft (In Progress)).
- Agent orchestration and agent-to-agent handoff.
- Any specific AI model, vendor, cloud, database or vector store.

**Terminology.**

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Context package** | The output structure of this standard (only). Its fields are open (M2-O2). |
| **Context item** | One unit inside a context package. Working term. |

The schema file name is kept for compatibility. |
| **Applicability result** | The output of [MAK-0003](MAK-0003.md), identified by `applicability_id`; the input to MAK-0002. Its fields are open (M2-O1). |
| **Conflict / candidate** | As in [MAK-0004 §A3](MAK-0004.md#a3-detection): two or more lineages claiming one subject; their heads are candidates. States `open`, `resolving`, `resolved`. |
| **Integrity error** | As in [MAK-0005 §A7](MAK-0005.md#a7-integrity): `zero_active`, `multiple_active`, `dangling_pointer`. |
| **`hidden_candidate_count`** | Count of conflict candidates the caller cannot read ([MAK-0004 §A6.4](MAK-0004.md#a6-retrieval-of-conflicted-subjects)). |

## 3. Settled design

Only the requirements below are agreed. Each restates a decision made in
a merged standard or by the owner; MAK-0002 does not add to them. Where a
requirement depends on [MAK-0009](MAK-0009.md), it is architecture-
defined and **not assessable** until MAK-0009 is bound and implemented
([MAK-0009 §8](MAK-0009.md#8-conformance-status-at-this-revision)).

**S1 — Boundary with MAK-0003.**
- MAK-0003 decides what applies, with rationale. Its inputs are the
  current knowledge result, history when explicitly requested, and
  MAK-0009 inherited knowledge, within the requester's authorization.
- MAK-0002 decides how the applicable knowledge is packaged. It MUST NOT
  re-run applicability determination or conflict detection.
- The interface between the two is the applicability result
  (`applicability_id`). Its fields are open (M2-O1).
- MAK-0002 MUST NOT parse the opaque `resolution_trace` of the current
  knowledge result to derive rationale
  ([MAK-0008 §10.5a](MAK-0008.md#10-tool-catalog)).

*Source: [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md#approved-future-protocol-scope) F-1/F-2
and ROADMAP criterion 8; shared boundary with [MAK-0003](MAK-0003.md).*

**S2 — Conflicts are preserved, never resolved here.**
- A context package MUST carry every open or resolving conflict it
  receives as a conflict, with all readable candidates.
- It MUST NOT select, merge or drop candidates, and MUST NOT present a
  candidate of an open or resolving conflict as current knowledge.
- Ordering, size reduction or any filter MUST NOT hide one side of a
  conflict.
- The package MUST make the conflicted state explicit.
- Rule keys reported as conflicted, or rule candidates reported as
  `blocked_by_mandatory`, are carried as such; no candidate is chosen and
  there is no fallback to an ancestor `default` (architecture-defined).

*Source: [MAK-0004 §A1, §A3.2, §A6.1–§A6.3](MAK-0004.md#a6-retrieval-of-conflicted-subjects);
[MAK-0009 §4.6–§4.7](MAK-0009.md#4-rules-enforcement-and-inheritance);
ROADMAP criterion 1.*

**S3 — Integrity errors are surfaced, never guessed.** A lineage with an
integrity error contributes no current record. A context package MUST
carry integrity errors it receives and MUST NOT substitute a guessed
current version.

*Source: [MAK-0005 §A7](MAK-0005.md#a7-integrity); ROADMAP criterion 1.*

**S4 — Lifecycle and history.**
- Governed records are `active`, `superseded` or `withdrawn`. Withdrawn
  and superseded versions are never current knowledge. Until MAK-0002 is
  complete, implementations MUST NOT inject them as current.
- History appears only when explicitly requested, labelled with its
  status and, where relevant, its `resolution_id`.
- Governed records have no `deprecated` state.
- Distributable artifacts are `active`, `deprecated` or `withdrawn`. A
  `deprecated` artifact still applies and SHOULD name its replacement in
  `deprecated_by`. A `withdrawn` artifact no longer applies to any
  project (architecture-defined).

*Source: [MAK-0005 §A6](MAK-0005.md#a6-lifecycle-states);
[MAK-0008 §10.6](MAK-0008.md#10-tool-catalog);
[MAK-0009 §2.2](MAK-0009.md#2-knowledge-profiles); ROADMAP criterion 4.*

**S5 — Disclosure.**
- A context package MUST NOT contain content the requesting principal
  may not read.
- A conflict candidate the principal cannot read MUST NOT be disclosed by
  identifier, title or content. Its existence is disclosed only as
  `hidden_candidate_count`.
- A principal without `read` on the project learns nothing: the request
  fails with `access_denied`, indistinguishable from a missing project.
- Per-record restrictions do not exist yet: every reader of a project can
  read all of its records, so `hidden_candidate_count` is 0 today. Rules
  about restricted records are forward-looking.

*Source: [MAK-0004 §A6.4](MAK-0004.md#a6-retrieval-of-conflicted-subjects)
and [D-007](MAK-0004.md#decision-d-007--existence-disclosure-for-restricted-conflicts);
[MAK-0006 §5.4–§5.5](MAK-0006.md#5-permissions-and-grants);
[MAK-0008 §9](MAK-0008.md#9-error-model); ROADMAP criterion 2.*

**S6 — Authorization.**
- A package is built for one authenticated principal (human, service or
  agent) whose effective permissions are its live project grant
  intersected with the authentication ceiling. Building one requires
  `read` on the project.
- One project per request, as for every MAK-0008 tool. Cross-project
  assembly is open (M2-O14).
- MAK-0002 applies authorization outcomes; it makes no authorization
  decision of its own.
- Organization knowledge reaches a project only through MAK-0009
  inheritance. Project `read` covers the distributable artifacts that
  apply to that project, and only those. Rule precedence MUST NOT bypass
  grants. Nothing crosses an organization boundary.

*Source: [MAK-0006 §5.3–§5.6](MAK-0006.md#5-permissions-and-grants);
[MAK-0008 §10](MAK-0008.md#10-tool-catalog);
[MAK-0009 §1.4, §4.8](MAK-0009.md#4-rules-enforcement-and-inheritance);
ROADMAP criterion 7.*

**S7 — Provenance.**
- Items carry the authenticated provenance object: `principal_id`,
  `principal_type`, `agent_id`, `display_name` (snapshot),
  `auth_method`, `credential_id` / `oauth_client_id`, `client`
  `{name, version, verified: false}`, `recorded_at`.
- Client metadata is never authorship and MUST NOT be presented as such.
- MAK-0001 `author` is packaging metadata of distributable artifacts
  only; it is not authenticated provenance.
- Legacy records without provenance are carried without fabricated
  provenance.

*Source: [MAK-0006 §7](MAK-0006.md#7-provenance);
[MAK-0005 §A1](MAK-0005.md#a1-scope-and-relationship-to-mak-0001);
ROADMAP criterion 3.*

**S8 — MAK-0009 knowledge (architecture-defined; not assessable yet).**
- Inherited distributable artifacts MUST keep their source identity
  (`organization_id`, `artifact_id`), `package_version` and provenance.
  They MUST NOT be re-attributed, re-versioned or presented as project
  records.
- **Effective rules are a required context category**, carried with their
  explanation: originating scope, enforcement, and the status of each
  rule on the chain (`effective`, `overridden`, `blocked_by_mandatory`,
  `override_not_declared`, `conflicted`).

*Source: [MAK-0009 §2.2, §4.6](MAK-0009.md#4-rules-enforcement-and-inheritance);
owner decision U-15 ([MAK-0009 Decisions](MAK-0009.md#decisions-2026-10-12));
ROADMAP F-1 ("current knowledge, effective rules and history");
ROADMAP criterion 12.*

**S9 — Versions are never compared.** A record revision and a package
version MUST NOT be compared, ordered or converted against each other.

*Source: [MAK-0009 §2.3](MAK-0009.md#2-knowledge-profiles).*

**S10 — Terminology.** "Context package" names only this standard's
output. The `mak4i_get_current` result is the "current knowledge result".
The MAK-0003 output is the "applicability result" (`applicability_id`).
`resolution_id` and "resolution" refer only to conflict resolution.

*Source: ROADMAP criteria 5 and 6;
[`context-package.schema.json`](../schemas/context-package.schema.json).*

**S11 — Delivery is out of scope.** Delivering a package into an AI
session is not specified, and "injection" is not a certifiable client
flow. CONFORMANCE.md has no criteria for MAK-0002.

*Source: ROADMAP criterion 11; [CONFORMANCE.md](../CONFORMANCE.md#drafts-in-progress-no-criteria).*

**S12 — No compliance guarantee.** MAK4I governs the content and
explanation of context. It cannot guarantee that an AI system follows
it, and implementations and documentation MUST NOT claim otherwise.

*Source: [MAK-0009 §4.9](MAK-0009.md#4-rules-enforcement-and-inheritance).*

## 5. Implementation status

No implementation may claim conformance to this document.

In the Reference implementation (talvikai/mak4i-reference, protocol pin
`62fca93`):

- `mak4i_get_current` returns the current knowledge result: current
  records, open and resolving conflicts with all readable candidates,
  integrity errors, an instruction not to choose a side, and optionally
  an opaque `resolution_trace`.
- `mak4i_search` applies deterministic filters: `artifact_type`, tags
  (all-of) and status (default `active`).
- `mak4i_history` returns all versions of a lineage.

There is **no** relevance or applicability ranking, task-based selection,
relationship expansion, size budget, truncation, inclusion rationale
beyond the opaque trace, uncertain-item handling, context package or
`inject` command, and **no** MAK-0009 inheritance or effective rules.
Today [MAK-0008 §10.6](MAK-0008.md#10-tool-catalog) is the only
injection rule.

## Appendix A — Design notes (non-normative)

*Non-normative. Describes alternatives and working ideas; nothing here is
a requirement.* The notes keep the substance of the v0.1 working draft,
rewritten so that nothing contradicts §3. Where a note mentions a settled
rule, it cites the S-number. Alternatives are listed side by side; none
is chosen.

### A.1 Processing model (illustrative)

```
Current knowledge result    History (explicit request)    MAK-0009 inherited
(records, conflicts,        (all versions, labelled        artifacts + effective
 integrity errors)           with status, resolution_id)   rules (architecture)
          \                          |                          /
           \_________________________|_________________________/
                                     v
                MAK-0003: what applies (within the principal's
                authorization; conflicts carried, not resolved)
                                     |
                         applicability result
                          (applicability_id)
                                     v
                MAK-0002: how it is packaged
                  1. place items into categories (A.3)
                  2. label status and role (A.4)
                  3. order by priority (A.5)
                  4. embed or reference (A.8)
                  5. apply the size budget; reduce (A.6, A.7)
                  6. attach provenance and rationale (S7, A.10)
                  7. record omissions, partiality (A.9)
                  8. handle uncertain items (A.13)
                  9. stamp format version and id (A.12)
                                     |
                              context package
                                     |
                (delivery into an AI session — out of scope, S11)
```

Steps 1–9 never add a candidate, remove a candidate or choose a winner
(S2), and never add content the principal cannot read (S5). Possible
request inputs (M2-O15): project; a task description; an optional size
budget; whether history is requested; and, once MAK-0009 is bound, a
target scope for effective rules (project, resource group or resource).

  "history_requested": false,
  "items": [
    {"artifact_id": "…", "revision": "2.0", "role": "current",
     "rationale": {"code": "…", "detail": "…"}}
  ],

  ],
  "integrity_errors": [{"lineage_id": "…", "kind": "zero_active"}],

### A.3 Category taxonomy (adapted from v0.1 §1; M2-O2)

v0.1 proposed 14 categories that every package could represent, each
possibly empty. Adapted to v0.2:

| Category | v0.2 source / note |
|---|---|
| Project identity | Organization and project of the request (S6). |
| Current project state | Current records not in conflict ([MAK-0004 §A6.1](MAK-0004.md#a6-retrieval-of-conflicted-subjects)). |
| Active decisions | Current records; "decision" is an open `artifact_type`, not a protocol kind. |
| Decision rationale | Record `rationale` field ([MAK-0005 §A2](MAK-0005.md#a2-record-fields)). |
| Active requirements / constraints | Current records; MAK-0009 resources can hold them. |
| Relevant artifacts | Whatever MAK-0003 marks applicable. |
| Artifact relationships | Lineage links (`supersedes`, `superseded_by`, `merged_from`); other relationship types are MAK-0003 open items. |
| Relevant history | Only on explicit request, labelled (S4). |
| Supersession information | Lineage pointers, without embedding superseded content. |
| Provenance | MAK-0006 §7 object per item (S7). |
| Applicable standards / policies | Replaced by **effective rules** (required, S8) and **inherited distributable artifacts** (S8). |
| Task-specific context | Supplied with the request (M2-O15). |
| References / identifiers | See A.8. |
| **Open conflicts** (new) | Required whenever present (S2). Includes `hidden_candidate_count`. |
| **Integrity errors** (new) | Required whenever present (S3). |
| **Uncertain items** (new, open) | Only if M2-O9 decides to represent them. |

Idea carried from v0.1: an empty category should be distinguishable from
a category whose content was omitted (for example by size reduction).

### A.4 Status and role labels (M2-O4)

v0.1 labelled every item `current` or `historical`. In v0.2 that is not
enough: a conflict candidate is neither current nor historical; a
`deprecated` distributable artifact still applies; an uncertain item is
not applicable. Alternatives:

- **One enum**, for example `current | historical | conflict_candidate |
  uncertain | integrity_error`, with lifecycle status carried separately.
- **Two axes**: the source lifecycle status as stored (`active`,
  `superseded`, `withdrawn`; or `active`, `deprecated`, `withdrawn` for
  distributable artifacts), and a package role (`current`, `history`,
  `conflict`, `uncertain`).

Whichever is chosen, S2 and S4 fix what may never be labelled
current.

### A.5 Ordering and priority (v0.1 §3, D-001; M2-O5)

v0.1 ideas: priority should be explicit on each item, not only implied by
position (position does not survive reformatting); and at minimum it
should distinguish "required to attempt the task correctly" from
"supporting/background". Alternatives:

- **A** — a small fixed set of named tiers (for example `critical`,
  `active`, `supporting`, `historical`).
- **B** — an open numeric rank; only relative order is meaningful.
- **C** — named tiers, each refined by a numeric rank.

Questions for v0.2: whether conflicts, integrity errors and `mandatory`
effective rules sit in a fixed top tier, or are ranked like other items;
and how MAK-0003 relevance signals map to tiers (joint).

### A.6 Size budget (v0.1 §6, D-002; M2-O6)

v0.1 ideas: the budget is an input to assembly; MAK-0002 never assumes a
model's context window; each item can declare or compute its size cost.
Unit alternatives:

- **A** — implementation-defined; the package records the unit used.
- **B** — an abstract, tokenizer-independent cost unit.
- **C** — a recommended default (for example UTF-8 bytes) with override.

MAK-0001 v0.2 defines `token_estimate` as the estimated size of
`content` in tokens, so reusing it as a size signal for distributable
artifacts is consistent. Project records have no such field; their cost
would be computed.

### A.7 Reduction and truncation (v0.1 §7, D-003; M2-O7)

v0.1 ideas, kept: reduction follows a declared, non-arbitrary strategy;
it respects priority (no lower-priority item stays in full while a
higher-priority one is reduced); an embedded item may first become a
reference; every reduced or excluded item is recorded (id and reason);
the within-tier order (oldest first, largest first, least relevant
first) is implementation-defined or standardized (open).

Constraints from §3: reduction can't drop or shorten one candidate of a
conflict while keeping another (S2). It can't remove the conflicted
marker. Whether a whole conflict may be carried as references only, and
whether effective rules may be reduced at all, are open.

### A.8 Embedded and referenced content (v0.1 §5, §11; M2-O18)

v0.1 principle, kept: a reference is a first-class outcome, not a
degraded fallback. An implementation may reference content that would
fit (for example content that changes often). Ideas for v0.2:

- A reference to a project record could be `artifact_id` plus revision.
  A reference to a distributable artifact could be (`organization_id`,
  `artifact_id`, `package_version`) (S8). The two never share an
  ordering (S9).
- Embedded copies keep enough metadata to trace them to their source.
- A reference that can't be resolved when needed (source unreachable,
  record not found) is marked unavailable, not treated as resolved.
  v0.1 also listed "access revoked". Under MAK-0006 §6.3, permissions are
  evaluated per request, so a principal that loses `read` gets
  `access_denied` for the whole request; it doesn't get a package with
  "unavailable" items (S5).
- Retry, caching and fallback stay outside the standard.

### A.9 Partial and insufficient context (v0.1 §12; M2-O13)

v0.1 ideas, kept:

- A package can declare itself **partial** and name the incomplete
  categories.
- A package not declared partial claims completeness only relative to its
  input, not to everything that might exist.
- **Insufficiency** is a separate axis: information necessary to attempt
  the task correctly or safely is missing. A partial package can be
  sufficient; a nominally complete one can be insufficient.
- The package carries insufficiency when it has been determined; it
  doesn't hide it.

Changed for v0.2: v0.1 kept authorization-caused omissions out of the
omissions list entirely. Now conflicts disclose `hidden_candidate_count`
(S5). Whether other restricted omissions are counted is M2-O10. Example
omission reasons: `truncated`, `unavailable`, `not_applicable_in_budget`,
`hidden_candidates` (count only).

### A.10 Inclusion rationale (v0.1 §13, D-005; M2-O8)

v0.1 ideas, kept:

- Each item can carry a short structured reason for its presence.
- Rationale traces back to the upstream applicability rationale
  (MAK-0003, via `applicability_id`) and is never rewritten into a new
  justification.
- MAK-0002 may add its own, separate reason for a *representation*
  choice (referenced, reduced).

v0.2 notes:

- v0.1 traced rationale "through MAK-0004 conflict resolution". That
  path no longer exists, because conflicts aren't resolved upstream of
  packaging (S2).
- A conflict is included because it is conflicted; it carries the
  MAK-0004 `reason` text (§A5).
- Effective rules carry MAK-0009's explanation (S8).
- The opaque `resolution_trace` is never parsed (S1).

### A.11 Reproducibility (v0.1 §10; M2-O12)

v0.1 idea: the same applicability result, budget and ordering rules
should give an equivalent package — the same items, order and rationale,
though not necessarily identical bytes. Any inherently non-deterministic
step (for example model-based deduplication) is declared as such and is
not presented as rule-based. MAK-0004 detection is deterministic
(§A3.2), so conflicts already meet this.

### A.12 Format version and assembly identifier (v0.1 §14; M2-O11)

v0.1 idea: a package declares the version of the MAK-0002 data model it
follows, in SemVer, plus an assembly-time identifier or timestamp.
Alternatives: one combined field, or two fields. A format version is a
third kind of version, distinct from record revisions and package
versions (S9). v0.1 called it "package version", which now collides
with the MAK-0009 term; another name (for example `format_version`) is
an open choice.

### A.13 Uncertain items (v0.1 §15, D-006; M2-O9)

Nothing is settled (a proposed direction is listed in M2-O9). v0.1 alternatives, kept:

- exclude uncertain items;
- represent them explicitly and distinguishably (for example a separate
  `uncertain` list, or a role value);
- trigger escalation or a clarification request outside the package.

v0.1 let uncertain items "bypass MAK-0004". In v0.2, conflict detection
already ran over all heads before any applicability step
([MAK-0004 §A3.2](MAK-0004.md#a3-detection)). So an uncertain item that
is a conflict candidate remains inside its conflict (S2). How
uncertainty is expressed (scale or flag) is joint with MAK-0003.

### A.15 Examples (rewritten from v0.1 §Examples)

**A. Coding task** — "Add rate limiting to the checkout service." The
package carries project identity, current records about the service's
architecture (embedded), a current constraint record (embedded), the
effective rules for the target scope (S8), and an inherited distributable
artifact holding a reusable rate-limiting pattern, as a reference with
its `package_version`. No history, since none was requested (S4).

**B. Architecture task** — "Propose the auth model for a new
multi-tenant service." Current decisions and their `rationale` rank high.
An organization tenancy-isolation rule appears as an effective rule with
`enforcement: mandatory` and its originating scope. It doesn't appear as
free text under "policies".

**C. Continuation by another AI** — "Pick up where the previous session
left off." Current records with rationale, and provenance showing which
principal and `agent_id` wrote each. The unverified `client` name is
shown only as such (S7). History is included only because the request
asks for it, labelled with status (S4).

**D. Small package** — A narrow question: project identity and task
context; every other category legitimately empty, not omitted.

**E. Large references** — A long design document and decision log are
carried as references; a third reference whose store was unreachable is
marked unavailable (A.8).

**F. Superseded decision** — "Use Platform A" was superseded by "Use
Platform B". The package embeds the current Platform B record. It points
to the superseded version by lineage pointer, without embedding it, and
never as current (S4).

**G. Open conflict (new)** — Two lineages claim `artifact_type=database,
subject_key=primary-store`. The package carries the conflict with both
candidates, their provenance, the MAK-0004 `reason`, and the instruction
not to choose a side. Neither candidate appears as a current record (S2).
Under size pressure the conflict is kept whole or referenced whole —
which of the two is open (M2-O7).

**H. Mandatory rule with blocked override (new, architecture-defined)** —
An organization `default` rule `logging.format` is overridden by a
project rule marked `mandatory`. A resource-level rule with the same key
is `blocked_by_mandatory`. The package carries the effective project rule
with its chain: the organization rule as `overridden` and the resource
rule as `blocked_by_mandatory`
([MAK-0009 Examples](MAK-0009.md#examples)).

### A.16 Security notes (from v0.1)

- Packaging must not be a path by which unreadable content reaches a
  principal, including through summaries or derived items (S5).
- Summarized or compressed content, if allowed (M2-O19), stays
  distinguishable from verbatim source content.
- Provenance is attached from stored, server-derived metadata, never read
  out of content bodies (S7, MAK-0006 §7.2).
- Content may contain instructions aimed at the AI system. The package
  labels the source and status of such content; it can't make an AI
  system ignore them (S12).
